Connect your email

Read-only connections. SmartNotes never sends email or opens attachments.

Initial sync reads the last 30 days, including Gmail Promotions and spam / Outlook Junk. Older mail, attachments, trash and sent mail are excluded.

Connecting an account sends its email text to the configured Gemini service for analysis. Disconnecting removes that account’s stored emails, tasks, and credentials from this app.
